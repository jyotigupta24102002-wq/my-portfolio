05 / inside my brain

I don't start with
"What should we post?"

The post is one output. The strategy comes first.

01

Understand the business

What are we trying to achieve — visibility, engagement, traffic, leads, sales or stronger brand recall?

02

Understand the audience

Who are we speaking to, what do they need, what do they care about and what makes them act?

03

Study the market

Competitors, search behaviour, platform patterns and existing performance shape the opportunity.

04

Build the strategy

Choose the right channel, message, content format, campaign structure and measurement plan.

05

Execute → measure → optimise

Launch the work, read the data, learn what worked and make the next decision better.

Then — and only then — do we ask what to post.
